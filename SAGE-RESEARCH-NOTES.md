# Sage research notes, read 11 Oct 2026 from sage.com/en-za (browser)

## Sage Accounting (accounting/ , accounting/pricing/ , features/*)
- Start R270/mo incl VAT (R3 240/yr, annual toggle = 12x). Single user. Features: create invoices in seconds, work anywhere, track what you are owed, get started quickly, supports 1 user. Sole traders, freelancers, micro businesses. Start also has stock management + invoicing (features page: "Available with Start and Accounting").
- Standard R490/mo incl VAT (R5 880/yr). 2 to 5 users. "Most popular". Everything in Start + AI features (draft bills from invoices, personalised customer emails), bookkeeping, calculate VAT returns, add up to 5 users, collaborate with accountant. Base package includes 2 users and 1 company. Receipts photo capture (AutoEntry): up to 99% accuracy (Sage claim).
- Add-ons monthly: extra users R85, extra company R460, Advanced Inventory R465, Debtors Manager R325, Time Tracking R185, Multi currency payments R185, Storage 1GB R20.
- Add-ons annual (features pages): users R840/yr, company R4 500/yr, Adv Inventory R4 620/yr, Debtors Manager R3 240/yr, Storage R204/yr. Time Tracking and Multi Currency "included on Option 3" (annual option 3).
- FAQ: "You can add as many users as you want" with roles read only to full. Upgrade/change/cancel any time. 30 days free trial, 30 day full refund. Sage 50cloud Pastel is "more expensive desktop accounting for advanced businesses".
- Bank feeds: direct feeds with First National Bank, Nedbank, Investec. Connect list: ABSA, FNB Business Profile direct feed, Standard Bank, Nedbank (New Online Banking), Capitec Bank, and more. Daily schedule check for new transactions. No extra cost for bank feed. Caveat: product may be supported but account type may not.
- VAT: monthly snapshot of VAT position; record of previous submissions; digital paper trail; enter SARS User ID to submit VAT returns directly from Accounting (VAT page). Domestic Reverse Charge VAT (valuable metals) page. Built for SA ("Developed and built for South African market", local support chat phone email, custom compliance DRC valuable precious metals, audit trails, robust inventory mgmt, advanced views for accountants).
- Reporting: sales, purchases, quotes, customer activity, VAT liability; cash flow statement and forecast; debtors and creditors ageing; export Excel CSV PDF; unreconciled transactions.
- Mobile app iOS and Android: quotes and estimates, stock levels, suppliers and contacts, debtors who owe most, overdue reminders.
- Stock: set reorder levels and quantities, out of stock warnings, best seller reports, categories and sub categories, upload items; Advanced Inventory = automatic continuous inventory + accounting updates, true cost of sales.
- Debtors Manager: automated workflow, timeline, draft follow ups, customer account status.
- Invoicing: recurring invoices, quotes to invoices, customised invoices, track viewed and paid, online payments (card payments, only available in SA).
- Membership perks: expert human support, Member Masterclass, Community Hub, business advice, learning, software certification.
- Switch pages exist: switch-to-sage, sage-vs-xero, sage-vs-quickbooks. Payroll standalone from R97.
- Customer story: Morningside Plumbing, South Africa.

## Sage 50cloud Pastel (products/sage-50cloud-pastel/)
- "Over 30 years experience", Sage says over 2 million businesses. Desktop installed on your PC, cloud connectivity (Remote Data Access), backups local or cloud automatically.
- Start: includes 2 users and 2 companies. Invoices and quotes, cash flow income expenses payments, discounts, automatic bank reconciliation. R1 375/mo excl VAT.
- Core: 4 users and 4 companies. Everything in Start + Supplier Payments, auto discounts, recurring invoices/transactions, bespoke reports, multiple companies departments budgets. R1 915/mo excl VAT.
- Plus: up to 5 users and 5 companies. Everything in Core. Extra users R265/mo. R2 715/mo excl VAT.
- Add-ons monthly excl VAT: foreign currency R460, Point of Sale R725, multiple warehouses and inventory R460, serial number tracking R460, Advanced Reporting R650, time and billing R460, GL Manager R725, Debtors Manager R595, Receipting R460.
- Features: live feed from bank, VAT compliance, reporting automation, stock (on hand, on order, movement, purchase orders), personalised dashboard, payments gateway.
- Trial: 30 days full desktop version; after joining get a new serial number and activation key, data stays.
- Aimed at small business with in house bookkeepers.

## Sage HR (hr/ , hr/pricing/)
- Core HR + Leave R28/employee/mo; Performance R14; Shift Scheduling R14; Timesheets R14; Expenses R10 (all per employee per month); Recruitment R1 850 per month (flat). Calculator on page. 30 day free trial ends automatically, no cancellation, demo data or own data, no payment details. No long term contracts.
- Best for SMEs. Mobile app free with a monthly subscription: time off, payslips and IRP5 certificates, shared calendar, feedback requests, expenses in multiple currencies; managers approve time off, publish payslips and IRP5.
- Modules: goals and OKRs, 1-to-1s, 360 feedback, surveys; timesheets with clock in/out, approvals; shift planning drag and drop; expenses with receipt snap; recruitment job ads and pipeline.
- Customers quoted: BetterSpace, Team Extension, Findasense, Sonarworks, Newscafe (SA), Marimba Jam (SA), Lingo24.

## Sage 200 Evolution (products/sage-200-evolution/)
