## Price on request

12 products give no price on their pages: Acumatica, LabourNet, NetSuite, PaySoft, PaySpace, Personio, Sage 200 Evolution, Sage CRM, Sage Pastel Payroll, Sage X3, SAP Business One, SYSPRO. The site says so, offers the guide to asking for a quote, and does not estimate a figure.

## Changes the vendors announced

- **Xero** says its subscription prices rise from 1 November 2026. The new figures were not published on 11 October 2026. The register shows the current prices and the blog carries a note.
- **Wave Accounting** is run from North America. Its card payment and payroll add ons serve US and Canadian businesses, and the listing says so.
- **BambooHR** and **PeopleHR** payroll add ons are for the United States and the United Kingdom. The listings say so.

## Products removed

- **Payroll Online** and **HR Companion**: no vendor website could be found to read a price or a feature from, so they are no longer listed.
